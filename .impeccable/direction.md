# Direction contract

## Scope and mode
Read-first study handbook, with Operate surfaces for search, practice and progress. User-approved implementation plan; visual direction is pinned by that plan. Code-first app screens, no raster comp.

## Audience and task
Glenelg Country School FBLA members find an event, read its focused lesson, then check recall. Maryland Central District is the default stage.

## Content and evidence
76 sourced events, original focused lessons and worked examples, explicit sources, season-specific requirements, independent practice questions. No invented mastery or placement claims.

## Visual world
A calm school study handbook: warm paper, dark navy typography, restrained teal actions, DM Sans interface and Source Serif reading titles. Thin rules separate a structured alphabetical directory. No decorative photo or stock dashboard card grid.

## First viewport
The header identifies GCS FBLA. A two-line editorial title, concise description and actual Central District date share the opening area. Search, preparation-format filters and the first event rows are visible immediately on desktop. Phones place the conference note above a compact search/filter directory.

## Signature interaction and constraints
A persistent desktop lesson contents list and a mobile disclosure let students jump to a concept. Native scrolling and clear anchors serve reading; optional topic checks support recall. All lessons are statically rendered and printable. Accessible controls, reduced motion, responsive measures, source conflicts and local-data limitations stay explicit.
