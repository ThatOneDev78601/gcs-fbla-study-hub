# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Delegated by the user and confirmed in the implementation plan: Astro, TypeScript, React islands, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui, Pagefind, static Sites hosting.

## Users
Glenelg Country School FBLA high-school students preparing for 2026–2027 competitions, primarily Maryland Central District.

## Product Purpose
Make each event easy to find and its published test objectives understandable through focused original lessons. A student should not need to read an entire textbook. Practice reinforces reading.

## Operating Context
District tests November 9–12, submissions October 31, Central District conference November 21, 2026; sourced and checked October 27. All 76 events, with district/state/national differences.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Readable topic lessons and quick reviews first; search, filters, quizzes, flashcards, rehearsal timers, checklists, local progress and export/import. No accounts. No private Drive credentials in the website. Source conflicts remain visible.

## Brand Commitments
Study-handbook design, warm neutral surfaces, navy and teal, strong typography and generous reading space. User approved the plan and delegated aesthetic implementation.

## Evidence on Hand
Current Maryland guides for all 76 events, national roster, MBA Research guides, public educational references. Historic Drive is supplementary and unverified. No fabricated outcome claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard-accessible, responsive, WCAG AA text contrast; readable without client JavaScript; reduced-motion support.
