## Use standards honestly

US GAAP and IFRS are reporting frameworks, not tax codes. Consistent recognition, disclosure, and separation of personal from business activity help users compare information. Estimates require judgment and evidence. Changing a bad-debt estimate merely to hit a target can mislead even when the books balance. Document methods and uncertainty rather than hiding inconvenient results.

Financial disclosure includes material information that could affect users’ decisions, often in statement notes. A reporting framework supports comparable recognition and presentation, while an independent assurance engagement evaluates evidence against criteria. Internal audit works within an organization’s governance system; an external audit provides an independent examination. Neither means management can transfer responsibility for accurate records to the auditor.

## Protect the audit trail

Separate payment authorization, cash custody, and recording. Reconcile the ledger to independent bank records. Software makes processing faster but can still process incorrect inputs. Use limited permissions, retained source documents, protected backups, and change logs. Distinguish timing differences from unrecorded book transactions; recording an outstanding check twice understates cash.

> **Work through an example**
>
> A bank statement contains a $12 fee missing from the ledger.
>
> Debit bank-fee expense and credit cash $12. An outstanding check already recorded in the ledger needs no duplicate expense entry.

## Know the tax record and career

Retain invoices, receipts, payroll records, asset details, and business-purpose evidence. Sales tax collected for government is generally a payable. Book income can differ from taxable income. Use current official instructions for real filings. CPAs work across public and other accounting roles; CMAs emphasize management accounting, CIAs internal audit, and CFEs fraud examination. Professional organizations provide education and standards. Audits examine evidence and provide assurance; they do not promise that every fraud will be found.

Keep tax records linked to the correct period and transaction. A sales-tax liability records tax collected; payroll liabilities record withheld amounts; an income-tax expense and payable concern the entity’s own tax obligation under the stated facts. Differences between financial and taxable income need investigation rather than a forced matching of unrelated totals.

## Key terms to remember

**Audit trail.** Evidence linking a transaction to its sources and changes.

**Reconciliation.** Comparing independent records and explaining differences.
