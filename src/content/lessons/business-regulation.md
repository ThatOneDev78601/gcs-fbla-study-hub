## Workplace obligations

Employment rules address nondiscrimination, wages and hours, safety, leave, labor relations, and privacy. Use job-related selection criteria, consistent processes, and documented evidence. Protect confidential records and respond to concerns through the proper process. Calling someone an independent contractor does not alone determine legal classification. The relevant jurisdiction, employer, relationship, and actual working facts matter.

OSHA concerns workplace safety and health; the Americans with Disabilities Act addresses disability-related nondiscrimination and accessibility obligations, including employment provisions. A reasonable-accommodation question requires attention to the relevant task and applicable standards rather than an automatic assumption about a person’s ability. Employment policies should provide a way to report harassment, safety hazards, and grievances without retaliation.

## Finance and consumer protection

Consumer protection addresses misleading claims and unfair treatment. Competition law concerns harmful restraints, including certain agreements among competitors. Financial institutions face identification, privacy, recordkeeping, and suspicious-activity obligations. Income, payroll, and sales taxes have different bases and responsibilities. Taxes collected for others are not ordinary earned revenue. Reliable records support both reporting and compliance; a book balance does not by itself prove legal compliance.

> **Work through an example**
>
> Two competitors agree never to discount below an identical minimum price.
>
> Coordinating prices raises a competition-law issue. It is not ordinary internal teamwork; seek the appropriate compliance guidance.

## International and ethical boundaries

International transactions can involve export controls, sanctions, customs, anti-bribery rules, and different contract systems. Evaluate a gift’s purpose, recipient, value, disclosure, and policy rather than assuming custom makes it acceptable. Legal compliance is a minimum; an allowed use of customer data may still violate a promise. Escalate uncertainty with facts. For actual decisions or filings, check current official rules rather than treating a short lesson as legal or tax advice.

Treaties are formal international agreements, while international custom reflects recognized practice and legal obligation. Customs procedures address goods crossing borders, including classification, valuation, origin, and documentation. A tariff is not the entire compliance process. Accurate records and assigned responsibility help prevent avoidable delays and false declarations.

## Key terms to remember

**Compliance.** Following applicable laws, regulations, and organizational requirements.

**Tax evasion.** Illegal tax avoidance through concealment or false reporting.
