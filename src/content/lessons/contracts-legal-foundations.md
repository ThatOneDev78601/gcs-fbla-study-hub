## Identify the law and claim

Constitutions establish government authority, legislatures enact statutes, agencies make delegated regulations, and courts interpret law. Civil disputes concern private rights and remedies; criminal proceedings involve government prosecution of offenses. A contract concerns a promised duty; a tort is a separate civil wrong. Negligence generally involves duty, breach, causation, and damage. Actual rules depend on jurisdiction and facts; these distinctions are for competition study.

The US has both federal and state court systems. A trial court hears evidence and determines facts; an appellate court reviews claimed errors under its authority rather than simply replaying the entire trial. A civil case can involve a complaint, response, discovery, motions, trial, and possible appeal. Mediation uses a neutral facilitator to seek agreement; arbitration uses a third party to decide under the parties’ arrangement.

## Test the agreement

An enforceable agreement typically requires offer, acceptance, consideration, capacity, and lawful purpose. Consideration is a bargained-for exchange, not a finding that the price was favorable. An invitation to negotiate is not automatically an offer. Duress, misrepresentation, incapacity, or illegality can affect enforcement. Some agreements must meet writing requirements. Distinguish void agreements from agreements a protected party can avoid.

> **Example**
>
> A printer offers 100 flyers for $40. The customer replies, “I will pay $30.”
>
> Changing the price proposes a counteroffer rather than accepting the $40 offer. Exchanging messages alone does not establish acceptance.

## Commercial rights and remedies

Goods, services, leases, agency, and negotiable instruments raise different questions. An agent acts for a principal within recognized authority. A warranty provides an assurance about a product or performance. Damages often compensate proven loss; specific performance is not the default for every breach. Trademarks identify commercial source, copyright protects original expression, and patents protect qualifying inventions. Buying a physical book does not transfer its copyright. Preserve agreements, communications, and delivery evidence before assessing a remedy.

Debtor-creditor law concerns obligations, collection, security interests, and remedies. A secured creditor has a claim linked to collateral under applicable rules; an unsecured creditor lacks that specific security. Environmental law concerns pollution, resource use, and related responsibilities. Consumer agencies address deceptive or unfair conduct; a product’s marketing claim should be supported rather than treated as acceptable because competitors repeat it.

## Key terms

**Consideration.** A bargained-for exchange.

**Agent.** A person acting on behalf of a principal.
