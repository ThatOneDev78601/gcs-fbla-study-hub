## Understand price decisions

Cost-based pricing starts with costs; competition-based pricing examines alternatives; value-based pricing considers what customers believe the benefits are worth. Demand, capacity, objectives, competition, and legal or ethical constraints all matter. A high price can signal quality but cannot create quality by itself. Transparent terms matter: a mandatory charge should not be hidden until the last step. Discounts change both volume and contribution, so evaluate their financial effect.

## Calculate with the right denominator

Markup on cost equals selling price minus cost, divided by cost. Gross margin percentage uses selling price as the denominator. Contribution per unit is price minus variable cost; it pays fixed costs before becoming operating profit. Break-even units equal fixed cost divided by unit contribution, rounded up when whole units are sold. Distinguish total revenue from profit and check whether a question asks for a percentage or dollar amount.

> **Example**
>
> A ticket sells for $20, costs $8 per attendee to deliver, and the event has $600 in fixed costs.
>
> Contribution is $12 per ticket, so break-even is 600/12 = 50 tickets. The $12 difference is 150% markup on the $8 cost but 60% margin on the $20 price.

## Choose a channel

A direct channel connects producer and buyer; an indirect channel uses intermediaries such as wholesalers, retailers, agents, or booking platforms. Intermediaries can provide assortment, storage, financing, discovery, and convenience. Evaluate reach, service, control, cost, data access, and customer preference. Technology can synchronize inventory and orders across channels. Channel conflict occurs when partners compete or disagree about price, territory, or responsibilities; clear agreements and consistent information reduce friction.

- Common mistake: calling every intermediary unnecessary because it receives a fee.
- Common mistake: using revenue instead of contribution in break-even calculations.
- Practice: compare a direct website with a reseller using total service and fulfillment cost.

## Key terms

**Contribution margin.** Selling price minus variable cost per unit.

**Intermediary.** An organization that helps move an offer from producer to customer.

**Break-even point.** The sales level at which total contribution equals fixed costs.
