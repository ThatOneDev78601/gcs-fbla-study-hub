## Know the authority and basic terms

Parliamentary procedure helps an assembly decide business fairly and efficiently, balancing majority decision with minority participation and individual rights. A quorum is the minimum required attendance to transact business. The chair presides, a member has the floor when recognized to speak, and a pending motion awaits disposal. Applicable law and governing documents take priority over default parliamentary rules. Maryland’s guide identifies Robert’s Rules of Order Newly Revised, 12th edition, for this event.

A useful hierarchy places applicable law and the corporate charter above bylaws, followed by special rules of order and the adopted parliamentary authority; standing rules address administration. Check the organization’s actual documents. One question at a time means the assembly resolves the immediately pending question before returning to the underlying business; it does not mean subsidiary motions can never interrupt a main motion.

## Use an orderly meeting

A standard order includes reading and approval of minutes, officer and board reports, committee reports, special orders, unfinished business and general orders, then new business. Minutes record what was done rather than a transcript of everything said. Correct minutes through the approval process. The chair maintains impartiality, states questions clearly, manages debate, and announces results. The secretary preserves the record. A special meeting is limited by its call and governing rules.

> **Work through an example**
>
> Several members leave and attendance falls below the quorum.
>
> Do not continue substantive decisions as if the original attendance permanently established a quorum. Follow permitted procedural steps and restore attendance before transacting business.

## Distinguish roles and documents

A president leads while presiding impartially; a vice president performs assigned duties and substitutes as appropriate; the treasurer reports finances; the secretary maintains records. “Pro tem” means serving temporarily, while “ex officio” means membership by virtue of another office. Voting rights depend on applicable rules; an organization can expressly create a nonvoting position. Executive session is confidential, not a license to disregard rules. Study the actual bylaws before assuming every organization follows an identical procedure.

Previous notice alerts members that specified business will be proposed under applicable timing and content rules. The notice can affect the vote required for actions such as rescinding. A notice requirement is not satisfied merely because someone privately mentioned an idea.

## Key terms to remember

**Quorum.** The minimum attendance required for conducting substantive business.

**Pro tem.** Serving temporarily in a role.
